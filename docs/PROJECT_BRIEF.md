# Project Brief: 3D Sports Store

## Overview
A premium, frontend-only sports e-commerce website for a business selling sports equipment and customizable sportswear.

## Target Customers
Individual athletes, students, sports clubs and teams ordering equipment or customized kits.

## Product Categories
Cricket, football, badminton, basketball, volleyball, tennis, sports footwear, apparel, protective equipment and accessories.

## Main Customer Journey
1. Browse sports categories.
2. Search and filter products.
3. Open a product details page.
4. Inspect available 3D models or product image galleries.
5. Select sizes, colors and customization options.
6. Add products to the cart or start a direct WhatsApp order.
7. Review the pre-filled WhatsApp message and manually send it.

## Custom Sportswear
Support player name, jersey number, size, color, logo upload where implemented, and team/bulk orders. Show a preview only to the extent supported by the available assets and implementation.

## 3D Experience
Use interactive GLB/GLTF viewers for products with suitable models. Products without 3D models must have image-gallery fallbacks. Do not promise that every product has a 3D model until assets are available.

## Scope
Frontend only: React, Vite and client-side state/localStorage. No backend, database, online payment processing or persistent admin product management in the initial release.

## Design Direction
Premium sports-brand aesthetic, large product imagery, strong typography, clear category navigation, polished cards, restrained motion, responsive layout and accessible interactions.

## Business Configuration
The business name, logo, currency, product data, policies and WhatsApp number must be configurable. Use clearly labelled placeholders until client details are supplied.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Core pages are responsive.
- Product browsing and selection work.
- Cart totals are correct.
- Customization details appear in the WhatsApp message.
- Missing 3D models do not break the product page.
- No fake payment, login or order-confirmation claims.
- The project builds successfully and setup instructions are documented.
